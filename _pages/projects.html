CoMET: Condition-Based Execution Monitoring

Ongoing research · Behaviour trees · Vision–language models · RLBench

Turning a successful demonstration into a task-specific monitoring plan. CoMET combines visual checks of expected task conditions with robot-state detectors to recognise manipulation failures, evaluated across 78 simulated tasks.

OfficeMate

Office assistant robot · TIAGo / ROS · HRI 2025

A social office assistant combining conversation, person-following, autonomous navigation, and activities such as snack handovers and breathing exercises. A seven-participant office pilot explored its potential to support well-being, alongside concerns about privacy, noise, communication, and interaction timing.
